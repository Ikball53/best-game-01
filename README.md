A fast-paced snake arena game. Eat glowing orbs to grow, boost past rivals,
trap other snakes so they crash into your body, and climb to the Top 3 with
2,500+ score to win. Fourteen rival snakes with their own colours, patterns and
personalities, a giant boss snake, gold / magnet / shield orbs, combo
multipliers, orb-rain events, a minimap, a kill feed and fully synthesized
WebAudio sound — no audio files, no network requests.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01

## How to play

- **Steer:** move the mouse, arrow keys / WASD, or drag on touch.
- **Boost:** hold SPACE, Shift, or click / hold touch — faster, but it costs length.
- **Eat orbs:** normal orbs grow you (+10), gold orbs are worth 50, magnet orbs
  pull nearby orbs to you, shield orbs save you from one crash.
- **Trap rivals:** cut across a snake's head so it crashes into your body. It
  bursts into orbs you can eat. Trapping the giant boss GARGANTUA triggers an
  orb explosion.
- **Win:** reach the Top 3 of the leaderboard. **Lose:** crash into another
  snake, yourself, or the arena wall (unless a shield saves you).
- **Mute:** press M or use the sound button. Your best score is saved locally.

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` — no build step,
no dependencies, no network requests.
